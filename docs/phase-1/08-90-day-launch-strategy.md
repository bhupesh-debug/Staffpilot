# StaffPilot — 90-Day Launch Strategy (Phase 1)

**Status:** Plan to execute. Targets are goals, not guarantees.
**Date:** November 22, 2026
**Goal:** 10 paying customers in 90 days from launch, each a 20–100-worker firm, each having completed (or being on track to complete) the core loop: timesheet → approval → billing → payroll-preparation estimate.

## 1. Shape of the plan

Three 30-day phases, each with a different job:

| Phase | Days | Job | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prove** | 1–30 | Discovery + first pilots | 15+ discovery interviews done; 3 pilot customers live on real data; core loop works for at least 1 pilot |
| **Convert** | 31–60 | Turn pilots into payers; scale outreach | 5 paying customers; pilot feedback folded into product and messaging |
| **Repeat** | 61–90 | Make acquisition repeatable | 10 paying customers; a written, repeatable outreach + onboarding playbook; churn = 0 so far (any churn investigated, not ignored) |

**Budget:** $0–$100 cash. All acquisition is direct outreach, referrals, and community presence — no paid ads in Phase 1.

## 2. Week-by-week plan

### Week 1 (Days 1–7) — Foundation
- [ ] Freeze Phase 1 strategy docs (this set) as the internal source of truth.
- [ ] Build the target list: 100 NY staffing/consulting firms in the 20–100-worker range (from public directories, LinkedIn, and local business listings). Record firm, size signal, owner/ops contact, and source. [UNVERIFIED — list quality to be judged by reply rates.]
- [ ] Finalize discovery script (`09-discovery-questions.md`) and outreach templates (`10-outreach-templates.md`).
- [ ] Set up a simple tracking sheet: firm → contact → outreach date → reply → interview → pilot → paying.
- [ ] Send the first 20 outreach messages (email + LinkedIn) to agency owners.

### Week 2 (Days 8–14) — First interviews
- [ ] Run 5+ discovery interviews. Take verbatim notes on pains, current tools, and willingness to pay.
- [ ] Send 20 more outreach messages. Follow up once on Week 1 non-replies.
- [ ] Log every interview finding against the personas (`02-personas.md`) — confirm or correct each persona.
- [ ] If interviews contradict the pricing tiers, note it immediately; do not wait until Week 4.

### Week 3 (Days 15–21) — First pilots
- [ ] Invite the 3 most engaged interviewees into a 30-day guided pilot (Growth-tier features, founder-led onboarding per `11-onboarding-workflow.md`).
- [ ] Onboard pilot 1 end-to-end: real worker records, real assignments, real timesheets.
- [ ] Continue outreach (20/week) and interviews (target: 10 cumulative).
- [ ] Fix the single biggest friction the first pilot hits — only that one; no feature sprawl.

### Week 4 (Days 22–30) — Prove the loop
- [ ] Pilot 1 completes a full cycle: timesheet → approval → invoice → payroll-prep estimate. Measure hours saved vs. their old process.
- [ ] Pilots 2–3 onboarded and running.
- [ ] 15+ interviews cumulative. Write the one-page "what we learned" summary: which pains are real, what firms pay today, what they'd pay StaffPilot.
- [ ] **Phase gate:** if no pilot has completed the loop, do not move to Convert — extend Prove by 2 weeks and diagnose why.

### Week 5 (Days 31–35) — Convert pilots
- [ ] Present each pilot with their measured result (hours saved, loop completed) and the proposed tier.
- [ ] Convert pilots to paying customers (target: 2–3 of the 3 pilots).
- [ ] Ask every converted customer for one referral introduction. Ask every non-converter why, in writing.
- [ ] Publish pricing page copy based on validated tiers (revise `05-pricing-model.md` first if discovery moved the numbers).

### Week 6 (Days 36–42) — Outreach engine
- [ ] Outreach volume to 30/week, using the best-performing template from Weeks 1–4.
- [ ] Start a second channel: one short, useful post per week in a NY staffing / small-business community (no ads, no spam — a genuine operational tip drawn from discovery findings).
- [ ] Onboard 2 more pilots from the new outreach batch.
- [ ] Target: 5 paying customers cumulative.

### Week 7 (Days 43–49) — Referrals and proof
- [ ] Systematize referrals: every paying customer gets a simple ask (one intro) and a thank-you.
- [ ] Collect the first written proof points (with permission): a quote about time saved, a before/after cycle-time figure. No quote is used without explicit permission; no logo without written permission.
- [ ] Fix the top onboarding friction from the first 5 paying customers (`11-onboarding-workflow.md` updated).

### Week 8 (Days 50–56) — Hold the line
- [ ] Paying customers: target 7 cumulative.
- [ ] Check in with every paying customer: is the loop running weekly? Any churn signal (silence, complaints, missed cycles)? Act on any signal within 48 hours.
- [ ] Review the revenue assumptions (`07-revenue-assumptions.md`) against actuals: real headcount, real tier mix, real sales cycle. Revise the document.
- [ ] If outreach reply rates are below ~5%, change the message or the list — don't just send more of the same.

### Week 9 (Days 57–63) — Repeatable motion
- [ ] Document the outreach → interview → pilot → paying playbook as actually executed (not as planned). This becomes the Phase 2 acquisition baseline.
- [ ] Onboard the next pilot batch (2–3 firms) from referrals + outreach.
- [ ] Paying customers: target 8–9 cumulative.

### Week 10 (Days 64–70) — Close the gap
- [ ] Focused conversion push on warm pilots and trials: personal follow-up, not bulk mail.
- [ ] Resolve any blocking product issue that is stopping a conversion. One fix, well-tested.
- [ ] Paying customers: target 10 cumulative — or a written diagnosis of why not, with the revised timeline.

### Week 11 (Days 71–77) — Stabilize
- [ ] Every paying customer has completed at least one full billing + payroll-prep cycle in StaffPilot.
- [ ] Onboarding checklist (`11-onboarding-workflow.md`) revised from all learnings.
- [ ] First churn-risk review: any customer not running a cycle in 2+ weeks gets a personal check-in.

### Week 12 (Days 78–90) — Review and next phase
- [ ] Final count vs. the 10-customer goal. Report honestly: what worked, what didn't, what the real numbers are.
- [ ] Revise all Phase 1 docs with actuals (personas, pricing, revenue assumptions).
- [ ] Decide Phase 2 scope (website + product hardening) from evidence, not from the original plan.
- [ ] Thank every pilot and customer personally. Ask the best 3 for a public testimonial (permission-gated) and a referral.

## 3. Weekly operating rhythm (all 12 weeks)

- **Monday:** send the week's outreach batch; review the tracking sheet.
- **Wednesday:** interviews and pilot onboarding calls.
- **Friday:** 30-minute review — interviews done, pilots live, paying count, top friction, one decision for next week. Update the tracking sheet and, monthly, the revenue assumptions.

## 4. Numbers to track weekly

| Metric | Week 1–4 target | Week 5–8 target | Week 9–12 target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outreach sent (cumulative) | 80 | 200 | 320 |
| Discovery interviews (cumulative) | 15 | 25 | 35 |
| Pilots live (cumulative) | 3 | 6 | 9 |
| Paying customers (cumulative) | 0–1 | 5 | 10 |
| Trial-to-paid conversion | — (too early) | measure | measure |
| Churn | 0 | 0 | investigate any |

All targets are goals, not forecasts. If a metric misses by a wide margin for two consecutive weeks, the plan changes — the target doesn't get quietly lowered without a note saying why.

## 5. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low reply rates to cold outreach | Test two message variants; use referrals as soon as the first customer lands; lead with a specific pain, not a feature list |
| Pilots stall (data migration too hard) | Founder-led onboarding; import from their spreadsheet rather than asking them to re-key; cap pilot scope to one client + one cycle first |
| Discovery contradicts the pricing model | Revise `05-pricing-model.md` immediately; re-test the new numbers before Phase 2 |
| A prospect asks StaffPilot to process payroll / file taxes | Decline clearly, explain the preparation-only boundary, offer the estimate workflow for their processor. Do not improvise into the boundary. |
| Founder time runs short | Cut outreach volume before cutting pilot onboarding quality — a stalled pilot costs more than a slow week of outreach |
| A customer asks for a feature that breaks the boundary (SSN storage, auto-classification) | Decline, explain why, log the request. Boundaries are not negotiable per-customer. |

## 6. What "done" looks like at Day 90

- 10 paying customers (or a written, honest account of the actual number and why).
- At least 5 of them having completed a full billing + payroll-prep cycle.
- A repeatable outreach → interview → pilot → paying playbook, written from evidence.
- Revised personas, pricing, and revenue assumptions grounded in real data.
- Zero boundary violations across the whole 90 days.
