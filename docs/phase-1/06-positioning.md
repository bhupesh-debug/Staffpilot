# StaffPilot — Positioning & Messaging (Phase 1)

**Status:** Draft messaging house for discovery validation. Use for internal alignment now; use publicly only after the proof points in `03-value-proposition.md` are gathered.
**Date:** November 22, 2026

## 1. Positioning statement

> **For** small staffing companies, consulting firms, and employers managing 20–100 workers in the U.S.,
> **StaffPilot is** the single platform for workforces, projects, and payroll preparation
> **that** connects assignments, timesheets, client approvals, billing, and payroll estimates in one workflow —
> **unlike** generic HR/payroll tools that don't model staffing economics, and enterprise staffing suites built for firms ten times larger,
> **StaffPilot** is sized, priced, and built for the way small staffing firms actually run.

## 2. Tagline

**"Workforces. Projects. Payroll. One Platform."**

### Tagline usage rules
- Always use the full tagline with all four beats. Do not shorten to "One Platform" alone — the three nouns are the positioning.
- "Payroll" in the tagline always means **payroll preparation (estimates)**. Wherever the tagline appears near product detail (website hero subcopy, pricing page, sales emails), the adjacent copy must say "payroll preparation" or "payroll estimates" — never bare "payroll processing."
- Do not pair the tagline with any claim of certification, compliance, or tax accuracy.
- Do not translate or paraphrase the tagline in sales copy without noting the canonical form.

## 3. Messaging house

### Roof (one-line promise)
One platform for your workforce, your projects, and your payroll preparation.

### Pillars (3 — each maps to a product area and a persona pain)

**Pillar 1 — See your margin.**
Assignments, bill rates, pay rates, and margin in one place — by client, consultant, and week.
*Audience:* Agency Owner, Consulting Ops Lead.
*Proof needed:* pilot customer margin report; time-to-answer measurement [UNVERIFIED until pilot].

**Pillar 2 — One approved hour, billed and prepped.**
Timesheets flow from entry → manager approval → client approval → invoice → payroll-preparation estimate, with no re-typing.
*Audience:* Payroll Manager, C2C Vendor Manager.
*Proof needed:* pilot customer completing a full cycle; hours saved per cycle, measured [UNVERIFIED until pilot].

**Pillar 3 — Built for staffing, sized for small firms.**
Assignment and vendor economics are the product, not an add-on — at a price and onboarding effort a 20–100-worker firm can actually adopt.
*Audience:* Agency Owner, C2C Vendor Manager.
*Proof needed:* competitor verification per `04-competitor-research-plan.md`; pricing validation per `05-pricing-model.md`.

### Foundation (trust — non-negotiable in every message)
- Payroll preparation is estimates only. StaffPilot never moves payroll funds, files taxes, or claims certifications.
- Your data is isolated to your firm. No cross-tenant access.
- No fake logos, testimonials, or statistics. Ever.

## 4. Voice and tone

- **Plain and direct.** Short sentences. No enterprise jargon ("synergy," "leverage," "end-to-end solution" without saying what it does).
- **Specific over grand.** "Invoices from approved timesheets" beats "transform your back office."
- **Honest about limits.** Saying "we prepare payroll estimates; your processor runs payroll" builds more trust than implying full payroll.
- **Written for the ops person, approved by the owner.** The buyer is the owner; the daily user is the payroll/ops lead. Copy must satisfy both: business outcome up top, workflow detail below.

## 5. What StaffPilot is NOT

Use this list in sales conversations and on the website FAQ. Saying what you're not is part of the positioning.

| StaffPilot is NOT… | …because |
|---|---|
| A payroll processor | It prepares estimates only; it never moves funds, makes deposits, or files taxes. |
| A PEO / employer of record | It does not employ anyone's workers. |
| A tax or compliance advisor | It does not calculate or guarantee taxes and does not classify workers. |
| A recruiting CRM | It tracks placements and assignments, not candidate sourcing pipelines. |
| An enterprise VMS | It is built for 20–100-worker firms, not 1,000-worker agencies. |
| A free spreadsheet replacement with no support | It is paid, supported software with an audit trail. |

- **Against generic payroll/HR SaaS:** "Those tools are built for direct employers. StaffPilot is built for firms that place workers at clients and earn a margin on the difference." *[UNVERIFIED — verify per `04-competitor-research-plan.md` before using publicly.]*
- **Against enterprise staffing suites:** "Those are built for large agencies with implementation projects. StaffPilot is self-serve, transparently priced, and sized for firms like yours." *[UNVERIFIED — verify per `04-competitor-research-plan.md` before using publicly.]*

## 7. Naming and consistency

- Product name: **StaffPilot** (one word, capital S and P).
- Tiers: **Starter / Growth / Scale** (see `05-pricing-model.md`).
- Personas: Agency Owner, Recruiter, Payroll Manager, C2C Vendor Manager, Consulting Ops Lead (see `02-personas.md`).
- Core loop language: **timesheet → approval → billing → payroll-prep estimate**. Use this phrase consistently across website, docs, and sales copy.
- Never use: "payroll processing," "we run payroll," "tax filing," "certified," "compliant" (as a claim about StaffPilot), or any customer name/logo without written permission.
