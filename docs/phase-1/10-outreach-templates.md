# StaffPilot — Outreach Templates (Phase 1)

**Status:** Working templates. Personalize every send with a real, checked detail about the firm. Never send a template verbatim to a firm you haven't looked at.
**Date:** November 22, 2026

## Rules for all outreach

1. **No fake claims.** No customer names, no testimonials, no statistics, no "we work with firms like yours" unless true. No competitor prices. No certification claims.
2. **One pain, one ask.** Each message names a single operational pain and asks for one thing: a 30-minute conversation.
3. **Personalize the first line.** Reference something real and verifiable about the firm (a role they staff, a location, a public listing). If you can't find anything real, don't invent it — use a plain, honest opener instead.
4. **Keep it short.** Cold email: under 150 words. LinkedIn: under 100 words.
5. **The boundary, in miniature.** If the message mentions payroll, it says "payroll preparation" or "payroll estimates" — never "we run payroll."
6. **Follow up once, then stop.** One polite follow-up after 4–5 business days. No third nudge unless they reply.
7. **Track everything.** Log firm, contact, date, template used, reply, and outcome in the tracking sheet.

## 1. Cold email — Agency Owner

**Subject options (pick one):** `Timesheets to invoice at [Firm Name]?` / `A question about how [Firm Name] tracks margin`

> Hi [First Name],
>
> I noticed [Firm Name] places [IT / finance] consultants across the New York area. Quick question: when a consultant's week is done, how long does it take for their approved hours to become an invoice — and do you see your margin per consultant without building a spreadsheet?
>
> I'm building StaffPilot, one platform for staffing firms where assignments, bill and pay rates, timesheets, client approvals, billing, and payroll-preparation estimates live together — one approved hour flows to the invoice and to the payroll estimate.
>
> Would you have 30 minutes this week to tell me how you run that workflow today? No pitch — I'm learning how firms like yours actually work before I finalize the product.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]
> StaffPilot — Workforces. Projects. Payroll. One Platform.

## What NOT to send

- Any message claiming customers, results, or statistics StaffPilot doesn't have yet.
- Any message saying StaffPilot "runs payroll," "handles taxes," or "is compliant/certified."
- Any message with a fake logo, a made-up testimonial, or a competitor's name used to imply endorsement.
- Bulk, unpersonalized blasts. If a message could have been sent to any firm, rewrite it.
