About

A small tool for small staffing firms, built honestly.

StaffPilot is a B2B software-as-a-service platform for staffing companies, consulting firms, and small employers managing 20–100 workers in the U.S. — New York first.

What we are building

Small staffing and consulting firms sit in a gap: spreadsheets that go stale, timesheets chased by email, billing and payroll preparation built from different sources, and generic HR/payroll tools built for direct employers — not for firms that place workers at clients and earn a margin on the difference. Enterprise staffing suites, meanwhile, are priced and implemented for firms far larger than 20–100 workers.

StaffPilot is the single platform for workforces, projects, and payroll preparation, sized, priced, and built for the way small staffing firms actually run: assignments with bill and pay rates, a timesheet → approval → billing → payroll-prep loop, and C2C vendor tracking.

Three lines we do not blur

SeparationWhat it means
Subscription softwareStaffPilot sells software access. It is not the customer’s employer, staffing agency, or payroll provider.
Staffing operationsThe customer runs their staffing business: hiring, placing, managing, and supervising workers. StaffPilot provides the workflow tools.
Payroll processingStaffPilot prepares estimates only. It never moves payroll funds, files taxes, or claims certifications.

How we work

  • Plain, direct language. Specific over grand.
  • Honest about limits — saying “we prepare payroll estimates; your processor runs payroll” builds more trust than implying full payroll.
  • No fake customer logos, testimonials, case studies, or market statistics. Ever.
  • Discovery first: pricing and positioning are validated with real firms before they are treated as final.

StaffPilot is an early-stage product. The website describes what the product does today and what is planned; planned items are labeled as planned. If you run a 20–100-worker firm and this workflow sounds like yours, talk to us.
