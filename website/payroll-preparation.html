Payroll preparation

Payroll preparation. Estimates only. Said plainly, on purpose.

StaffPilot prepares payroll estimates from approved hours and pay rates — figures for review, to hand to your actual payroll processor.

Read this first. StaffPilot does not run payroll. It does not move payroll funds, make direct deposits, file taxes, make tax deposits, or calculate or guarantee tax withholdings. It does not store real Social Security numbers or bank account details. It claims no payroll, tax, or compliance certification. Every payroll figure in the product is labeled an estimate.

What payroll preparation is

After timesheets are approved, StaffPilot computes what payroll would look like from those approved hours and the pay rates on each assignment: gross pay estimates by worker, by client, and by pay cycle. You review every line against approved hours and rates, then hand the figures to your payroll processor — the company that actually moves funds and files taxes.

How it works

Approved hours

Only manager- and client-approved, locked hours feed payroll preparation.

Pay rates applied

The pay rate on each assignment is applied to its approved hours.

Estimate generated

Gross pay estimates by worker and cycle, every figure labeled “estimate”.

You review

Check every line before it leaves StaffPilot. Nothing is final until you say so.

Hand to your processor

Export the run (Growth and Scale) and give it to the payroll processor that runs your payroll.

What it is not

  • Payroll processing — no funds movement, no direct deposits.
  • Tax filing or tax deposits — no tax calculation or guarantee.
  • A source of tax or compliance advice, or any certification.
  • A place to store Social Security numbers or bank details — StaffPilot does not ask for them.

Real tax calculation, if it ever comes to StaffPilot, would come only from a validated provider integration in a later phase — not from StaffPilot itself. That integration does not exist today.
