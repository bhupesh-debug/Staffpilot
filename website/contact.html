Before you write

  • Fit: StaffPilot is built for firms managing 20–100 workers. Smaller or much larger? Tell us anyway — we will say plainly whether it fits.
  • Trial: 14 days, no credit card, full Growth features. Request a demo to start.
  • Payroll: estimates only. Read the boundary before you ask us to run payroll — we can’t, and we won’t pretend otherwise.
  • Security: see the security information page for what is implemented and planned.

What not to send

Never send Social Security numbers, bank routing or account numbers, passwords, or worker tax documents through this form or by email. StaffPilot does not need them and does not store them.
