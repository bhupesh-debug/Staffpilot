Log in

Sign in to the StaffPilot application.

StaffPilot accounts sign in through the application itself, not through this website.

How sign-in works

Authentication for the StaffPilot application is provided by Supabase Auth. When you sign in to the application, you sign in with the email and password (or other method) set up for your firm’s tenant. Your firm’s data is isolated to your tenant, and your role determines what you can see and do.

This public website does not have a sign-in form and does not accept passwords. That is deliberate: credentials belong only in the application, never on a marketing page.

Application sign-in is not yet published. The StaffPilot application is still being built, and its hosted sign-in page is not live yet. This page will link directly to it as soon as it is. If you are an existing trial or pilot customer, use the sign-in link sent to you by the StaffPilot team.

New here?

If your firm does not have a StaffPilot account yet, start with the 14-day free trial (no credit card, full Growth features) or request a demo first. Signup creates your firm’s tenant and your admin user, and confirms in writing that StaffPilot prepares payroll estimates only.

Locked out?

Password resets and account recovery are handled inside the application’s Supabase Auth flow once it is live. Until then, contact us and we will help. Never share your password by email or through the contact form.

Security notes

See the security information page for what is implemented and planned, including tenant isolation and role-based access. StaffPilot holds no SOC 2 or ISO certification and claims none.
