How it works

One approved hour, billed and prepped

The core loop: a timesheet is entered once, approved, invoiced, and used to prepare payroll estimates. No re-typing, no second source of truth.

Enter timesheets

Consultants log weekly hours against their assignment — direct or vendor-supplied.

Approve

Manager approval, then client approval. Approved hours lock, with an audit trail of who approved what and when.

Bill the client

Invoices are generated from approved timesheets and bill rates — not re-keyed from email.

Prepare payroll

Payroll-preparation estimates are built from the same approved hours and pay rates, ready to hand to your payroll processor.

See your margin

Bill minus pay, by client, consultant, and week — from the same records, not a month-end spreadsheet.

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, including the limits

Does StaffPilot run payroll?

No. StaffPilot prepares payroll estimates from approved hours and pay rates. It never moves payroll funds, makes direct deposits, files taxes, or makes tax deposits. You hand the estimates to your actual payroll processor.

How does the 14-day trial work?

Fourteen days, no credit card required, with full Growth-tier features. At the end you can pick a tier or export your data and leave. No card was taken, so there is nothing to cancel and no auto-charge.

What does it cost for my firm?

A base fee plus a per-worker monthly charge: Starter $49 + $4/worker, Growth $99 + $6/worker, Scale $199 + $8/worker. At 45 workers that is $229, $369, or $559 per month. Use the calculator on the pricing page.

Does StaffPilot store Social Security numbers or bank details?

No. StaffPilot does not ask for or store real Social Security numbers or bank account details for payroll purposes.
