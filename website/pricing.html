Pricing

Transparent pricing you can work out in under a minute.

A base platform fee plus a per-worker monthly charge. No per-payroll-run fees, no per-invoice fees, no hidden payroll-processing fees — StaffPilot does not process payroll.

Price it for your firm

A “worker” is any active consultant/employee record on an assignment in the billing month. Inactive or archived workers don’t count.

Starter: $229/mo
Growth: $369/mo
Scale: $559/mo
Starter

$49/mo base + $4/worker/mo

For a small agency replacing spreadsheets with its first real system. Target: 20–30 workers.

  • Core worker records & assignments (bill rate, pay rate, margin)
  • Timesheets: entry, submit, manager approval
  • Client approval of timesheets
  • Invoicing from approved timesheets
  • Payroll-preparation estimates (labeled estimates)
  • Basic margin reporting (by client, by consultant)
  • 2 user seats included; extra seats $10/mo each
  • Email support
  • C2C vendor management module
  • Advanced reporting, API access, priority support

Examples: 25 workers $149/mo · 45 workers $229/mo · 50 workers $249/mo · 100 workers $449/mo.

Scale

$199/mo base + $8/worker/mo

For firms at the top of the segment running multi-client, multi-vendor operations. Target: 75–100+ workers.

  • Everything in Growth, plus:
  • API access (read/write, API keys, rate limits)
  • 10 user seats included; extra seats $10/mo each

  • Priority support, same-business-day target

Examples: 25 workers $399/mo · 45 workers $559/mo · 50 workers $599/mo · 100 workers $999/mo.

Talk to us about Scale

Free trial

14-day free trial, no credit card required. Full Growth-tier features during the trial, so you evaluate the real product, including vendor tracking. One tenant, up to 100 workers, no API access during the trial. At the end, pick a tier or export your data and leave. No card was taken, so there is no auto-charge.

What is never charged for

  • Payroll-preparation runs: unlimited, all tiers.
  • Timesheet submissions and approvals: unlimited.
  • Invoices generated: unlimited.
  • Data export on Growth/Scale: unlimited.

What no tier includes

  • Real payroll processing: no funds movement, no direct deposits, no tax filing, no tax deposits.
  • Storage of real SSNs or bank account details.
  • Worker classification advice or auto-classification.
  • Any certification claim.
  • Custom development.

Prices are proposed pricing, published for transparency and subject to change. Annual billing: 2 months free (about 17% off). The worker-count bands in the tier descriptions are a soft guide, not a hard cap — a Starter customer with 28 workers pays the per-worker rate for workers above 25 rather than being forced to upgrade. The tier difference is features, not headcount walls.
