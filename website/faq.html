FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers — including the limits. If your question is not here, contact us.

What is StaffPilot?

StaffPilot is a subscription software platform for small staffing companies, consulting firms, and employers managing 20–100 workers. It brings together worker records, assignments, timesheets, client approvals, billing, and payroll-preparation estimates in one tenant-isolated workspace per firm.

Does StaffPilot run payroll?

No. StaffPilot prepares payroll estimates only, from approved hours and pay rates. It never moves payroll funds, makes direct deposits, files taxes, makes tax deposits, or calculates or guarantees tax withholdings. You hand the estimates to your actual payroll processor.

What does it cost?

A base fee plus a per-worker monthly charge: Starter $49/mo + $4/worker, Growth $99/mo + $6/worker, Scale $199/mo + $8/worker. At 45 workers that is $229, $369, or $559 per month. Annual billing is 2 months free (about 17% off). See the pricing page for the full comparison and a calculator.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Fourteen days, no credit card required, with full Growth-tier features. One tenant, up to 100 workers, no API access during the trial. At the end you pick a tier or export your data and leave. No card was taken, so there is no auto-charge.

What counts as a “worker” for billing?

Any active consultant or employee record on an assignment in the billing month. Inactive or archived workers don’t count.

What is C2C, and how does StaffPilot handle it?

C2C (corp-to-corp) means consultants employed by a third-party vendor and placed at your clients. StaffPilot tracks vendor records, vendor consultants, vendor rates, and vendor invoices in the same workflow as direct consultants (Growth and Scale tiers). StaffPilot never auto-classifies a C2C consultant as an independent contractor — classification is your legal determination, made with your own counsel.

Does StaffPilot store Social Security numbers or bank details?

No. StaffPilot does not ask for or store real Social Security numbers or bank account details for payroll purposes.

Does StaffPilot classify workers or give tax advice?

No. StaffPilot does not classify workers, advise on classification, calculate taxes, or give tax or compliance advice. It records the engagement type you enter.

Is my firm’s data kept separate from other firms?

Yes. Each customer gets a tenant-isolated workspace, and role-based access controls who inside your firm can see and do what. No cross-tenant access. See the security information page for what is implemented and what is planned.

Is StaffPilot SOC 2 or ISO certified?

No. StaffPilot holds no SOC 2, ISO, tax, or payroll certification, and claims none. The security page states what is implemented and planned honestly, and will be updated if a certification is ever obtained.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export your billing and payroll-preparation data (Growth and Scale) and leave. At the end of a trial, the same applies: pick a tier, or export your data and leave.

Does StaffPilot integrate with QuickBooks or Xero?

Not yet. QuickBooks and Xero integrations are planned future integrations, with no date promised. The Scale API is the integration route available today, and data export (CSV/Excel) is available on Growth and Scale.

What if my firm has fewer than 20 or more than 100 workers?

StaffPilot is built and priced for 20–100 workers. If you are outside that range, contact us — we will say plainly whether it fits, rather than sell you a tool built for a different size of firm.

Can I get a demo before the trial?

Yes. Request a demo and we will walk your own workflow through the timesheet → approval → billing → payroll-prep loop in about 30 minutes.

Who do I contact with a question?

Use the contact page. Do not send Social Security numbers, bank details, passwords, or worker tax documents through the form or by email — StaffPilot never asks for them.
