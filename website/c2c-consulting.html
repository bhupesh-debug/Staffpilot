C2C consulting

Every vendor consultant, one view.

Corp-to-corp consultants are easy to lose track of: vendor hours, vendor rates, and vendor invoices in separate spreadsheets per vendor. StaffPilot tracks them in the same workflow as your direct consultants.

Vendor records

Vendor companies, contacts, agreements, and rate confirmations kept organized and retrievable — not scattered across email.

Vendor consultants on assignments

Vendor-supplied consultants sit on the same assignments, with the same timesheet and approval workflow as direct consultants.

Vendor rates & margin

Bill rate, vendor pay rate, and margin on vendor-supplied consultants — not just on direct ones.

Vendor invoices

Vendor invoices tracked against approved vendor hours, so vendor billing disputes have a shared approval record to check.

Classification is your determination, not ours. StaffPilot never auto-classifies a C2C consultant as an independent contractor. Classification is the customer’s legal determination, made with their own counsel. StaffPilot records the engagement type you enter and keeps the documentation organized — it does not advise on classification.
