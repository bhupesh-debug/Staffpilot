Features

Everything the weekly loop needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Worker records, assignments, timesheets, approvals, billing, payroll-preparation estimates, and reporting — in one tenant-isolated workspace per firm.

Worker & consultant records

Profiles, assignments, rates, documents, and status for every consultant and employee. Availability and roll-off dates visible to the people who place work.

Assignments

Worker, client, and project linked in one assignment, with bill rate, pay rate, and margin. Rate changes are audit-logged.

Timesheets

Weekly entry against an assignment. Draft → submitted → manager-approved → client-approved, locked after approval.

Client approvals

Clients review and approve submitted hours in the platform — no reply-all approval threads, and a clean record when hours are disputed.

Billing & invoicing

Invoices generated from approved timesheets and bill rates. Billing can never drift from approved hours, because it is built from them.

Payroll preparation (estimates)

Payroll-preparation estimates from approved hours and pay rates, labeled as estimates on every screen, ready to hand to your payroll processor. Learn what this means.

C2C vendor tracking Growth+

Vendor records, vendor consultants, vendor rates, and vendor invoices alongside direct consultants. C2C consulting.

Margin & utilization reporting

Margin by client, consultant, and week; utilization and billing-vs.-payroll-prep reconciliation on Growth and Scale.

Roles & permissions

Role-based access for owners, recruiters, payroll managers, and client approvers. Custom roles and permissions on Growth and Scale.

Data export Growth+

Export billing and payroll-preparation runs as CSV/Excel, so your data — and your exit — are never locked in.

Audit trail

Timesheet approvals, rate changes, and payroll-preparation runs are logged with who and when.

API access Scale

Read/write API with API keys and rate limits, for firms that need to connect StaffPilot to their other tools.

Planned, not yet available: QuickBooks and Xero integrations are planned future integrations. They are not available in this version, and no date is promised here. The Scale API is the integration route available today.

What StaffPilot deliberately does not do

  • Move payroll funds or make direct deposits.
  • File taxes or make tax deposits, or calculate or guarantee tax withholdings.
  • Store real Social Security numbers or bank account details.
  • Auto-classify C2C consultants as independent contractors — classification is your legal determination, made with your own counsel.
  • Claim any payroll, tax, or compliance certification.
