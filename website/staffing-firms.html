For staffing firms

Built for firms that place workers — and earn a margin on the difference.

You place consultants at clients, bill one rate, pay another, and live on the margin between them. StaffPilot models that economics as the product, not as an add-on.

The problems this solves

Margin blindness

Bill vs. pay rates live in spreadsheets. Nobody can say this week’s true margin without manual work. StaffPilot shows margin by client, consultant, and week from the same assignment records.

Timesheet chasing

Hours arrive by email and chat; client approval is a reply-all thread; disputes surface at invoice time. One workflow collects, approves, and locks hours — with an audit trail.

Billing and payroll prep don’t connect

Invoicing is built from one source, payroll preparation from another, and the margin is reconciled at month-end — if at all. In StaffPilot, one approved hour feeds both billing and the payroll-preparation estimate.

Slow handover after placement

Recruiters lose placements when handover to ops is manual and error-prone. A placement becomes an assignment with rates attached — no re-keying.

Who uses it in your firm

PersonWhat they get
Agency ownerMargin by client, consultant, and week — without asking ops to build a spreadsheet.
RecruiterWho is available, at what rate, and when they roll off.
Payroll & billing leadEvery timesheet in one place, approved, before the billing deadline; estimates that tie exactly to approved hours.
C2C vendor managerEvery vendor consultant, assignment, rate, and vendor invoice in one view. C2C consulting.

Honest limit: StaffPilot is not a recruiting CRM. It tracks placements and assignments, not candidate sourcing pipelines. And it prepares payroll estimates only — your payroll processor still runs payroll.
