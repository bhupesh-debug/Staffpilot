Privacy policy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: November 2026. This policy describes what StaffPilot collects on this website and in the product, and what it does not collect.

1. Who we are

StaffPilot is a B2B software-as-a-service platform for staffing companies, consulting firms, and small employers managing 20–100 workers. This policy covers the public website (this site) and the StaffPilot application.

2. What we collect on this website

When you use the demo-request or contact forms on this site, we collect the information you enter: your name, work email, company, firm type, worker count, and your message. In this version of the site, form submissions are stored only in your browser’s local storage until the API exists (Phase 15); they are not sent to a server. Once the API exists, submissions will be sent to StaffPilot so we can respond, and this policy will be updated to say so.

3. What the product collects

The application collects the business data your firm enters to run its staffing workflow: worker and consultant records, assignments, rates, timesheets, approvals, invoices, and payroll-preparation estimates. Your firm’s data is isolated to your tenant; no other customer can see it.

4. What we do not collect

StaffPilot does not ask for or store real Social Security numbers or bank account details for payroll purposes, and you should never enter them into the product or send them through the website forms or by email. StaffPilot does not sell personal data.

5. How we use information

We use contact information to respond to demo requests and enquiries, to provide the service, and to operate and secure it. We do not use your firm’s business data for advertising, and we do not share it with other customers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Contact and demo-request data is kept only as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the enquiry. Product data is kept while your subscription (or trial) is active. You may request export or deletion of your firm’s data by contacting us; Growth and Scale tiers also include self-serve data export.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no third-party analytics or advertising trackers. The only browser storage used is local storage for form submissions in this version (see section 2), which stays in your browser.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the product or the website changes how it handles data — including when the demo-request and contact APIs go live. The “last updated” date at the top will change with it.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact page.
