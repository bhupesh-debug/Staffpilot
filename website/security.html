Security information

Security, stated honestly.

What StaffPilot does to protect your firm’s data, what is planned, and what we do not claim.

No certifications held. StaffPilot holds no SOC 2, ISO 27001, tax, or payroll certification, and claims none. If a certification is ever obtained, this page will be updated to name it, with the date. Do not take any statement on this page as a certification claim.

What is in place or planned

AreaStatusDetail
Tenant isolationPlanned architectureEach customer’s data lives in its own tenant. Isolation is enforced at the database level with PostgreSQL row-level security (RLS) in the application design, so no customer can see another’s data.
AuthenticationPlanned / in buildSign-in is provided by Supabase Auth in the application. Passwords are handled by that provider, not stored by StaffPilot’s own code.
Encryption in transitProvided by the platformTraffic to the application and website is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS) via the hosting platform.
Encryption at restProvided by the platformDatabase storage encryption is provided by the database hosting platform (Supabase/PostgreSQL).
Role-based accessPlanned / in buildOwner, recruiter, payroll manager, and client-approver roles, with custom roles on Growth and Scale. Permissions gate what each role can see and do.
Audit trailPlanned / in buildTimesheet approvals, rate changes, and payroll-preparation runs are logged with who and when.
BackupsProvided by the platformDatabase backups are provided by the database hosting platform; restore procedures are part of the application’s operations plan.
SOC 2 / ISO 27001Not heldNo certification has been obtained. Not claimed, not implied.

Status labels are deliberate: “planned / in build” means designed and being implemented, not yet independently verified in production. This page will be updated as items move from planned to live and verified.

What StaffPilot never stores

  • Real Social Security numbers.
  • Bank routing or account numbers for payroll purposes.
  • Passwords on the public website — this site has no sign-in form.

Your part

  • Give each user their own account and the smallest role they need.
  • Never share passwords, and never send SSNs or bank details to us by email or form.
  • Tell us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access to your tenant, via the contact page.
