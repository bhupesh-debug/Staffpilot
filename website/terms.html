Terms of service

Terms of service.

Last updated: November 2026. These terms cover use of the StaffPilot website and the StaffPilot application.

1. The service

StaffPilot provides subscription access to software for workforce, project, and payroll-preparation workflows. StaffPilot is not the customer’s employer, staffing agency, or payroll provider.

2. Payroll preparation — estimates only

StaffPilot prepares payroll estimates from approved hours and pay rates. It does not move payroll funds, make direct deposits, file taxes, make tax deposits, or calculate or guarantee tax withholdings. Payroll figures in the product are labeled estimates. The customer is responsible for reviewing every estimate and for all actual payroll processing, tax filing, and payments through its own payroll processor.

3. What the customer is responsible for

The customer runs its own staffing business: hiring, placing, managing, and supervising its workers and consultants, and all worker classification determinations (made with its own counsel). The customer must not enter real Social Security numbers or bank account details into the product. The customer is responsible for the accuracy of the rates, hours, and records it enters.

4. Subscriptions, trial, and pricing

Subscriptions are billed per the published tiers (Starter, Growth, Scale) at a base fee plus a per-worker monthly charge. The 14-day free trial requires no credit card and includes full Growth features; no card is taken, so there is no auto-charge at trial end. Prices are proposed pricing, published for transparency, and may change; existing customers will be told of a price change before it applies to them.

5. Acceptable use

You must not use the service to break the law, to misrepresent payroll estimates as final payroll or tax figures, to store Social Security numbers or bank details, or to attempt to access another customer’s tenant. We may suspend access for misuse.

6. Data and confidentiality

Each customer’s data is isolated to its tenant. We do not share one customer’s data with another. See the privacy policy and the security information page.

7. No certifications; no warranties beyond the service

StaffPilot claims no SOC 2, ISO, tax, or payroll certification. The service is provided as a workflow and preparation tool; payroll figures are estimates, not final pay or tax figures, and StaffPilot gives no tax or legal advice.

8. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, StaffPilot’s liability is limited to the subscription fees paid for the service in the period in question. StaffPilot is not liable for payroll, tax, or employment decisions the customer makes on the basis of estimates.

9. Changes

We may update these terms as the product develops; the “last updated” date at the top will change, and material changes will be flagged to customers.
